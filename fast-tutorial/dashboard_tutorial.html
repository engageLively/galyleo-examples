Global Weather Dashboard

In Tutorial 1, you published the global_weather_2023.sdml dataset. Now, let's visualize the temperature trends using a dynamic city filter.

1 Open the Editor

Click the New Galyleo Dashboard Icon in the Launcher.

2 Verify Data (Optional)

Click on "Tables" in the right sidebar to confirm global_weather_tutorial is loaded.

3 Create the Filter

Click on "Filters" in the right sidebar and select "Add Filter".

In the dialog, configure the following:

  • Name: CityChooser
  • Type: Dropdown
  • Table: global_weather_tutorial
  • Column: CITY

A menu will appear in the top left with city names

4 Create the View

Click on "Views" in the right sidebar and select "Add View".

In the dialog, configure the following:

  • Name: CityTemp
  • Table: global_weather_tutorial

An editor will pop up. Select "DATE" and "TEMP" as columns and choose CityChooser as the filter. Click "Update View".

5 Create the Chart

Click on "Chart" in the right sidebar and select "Add Chart".

In the dialog, configure the following:

  • Name: CityTemp
  • dropdown: CityTemp (it will be the bottom item of the menu)

An editor will pop up. This editor will look familiar; it is exactly the same chart editor used in Google Sheets.

Select the chart type you want. "Area Chart" is a good choice for this. The preview will change to match the chart you select.

Click OK. The chart will appear in the top left. right-click it so a Halo of tools appears around the chart.

The cross icon is the drag tool. Click on it, hold the mouse down, and use it to drag the Area Chart into positio.

6 Play With the Chart

Click on the editing/interacting tool in the top bar (arrow on the far left). A menu will appear. Choose interacting and the icon will change to a hand

Now choose a city from the CityChooser you created. The temperature chart will change as you change cities.

7 Publish the Chart

Click on the Publish button in the top bar.

A dialog will appear with the names of previously published dashboards and a text box for the name. Put "citytemp.gd.json" in the textbox (the name must end in '.gd.json' and should have no spaces)

Click OK. A popup will appear with links for you to view your published dashboard.

Click on the link and your published dashboard will appear in a new browser tab.
